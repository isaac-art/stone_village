Isaac Clarke

I am currently a PhD candidate in Computational Media and Arts at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). I will be defending my thesis in Summer 2026.

My interests are in contemporary art, alien intelligences, and artists making their own tools.

Recent work can be found on my github.

Tom Petty's Quantum AI Alignment Jacket, 2025 An embroidered jacket with embedded BLE micro-contollers that I use to steer control vectors of language models and control audio playback. Based on the suit Tom Petty wore at Live Aid 1985.

Some other recent works are BackChat and A Speculative AI Reenactment of the Figurists' Encounters with the I Ching

Previously I was involved in: The Institute of Jamais Vu, London | Serf, Leeds | NL'CAO, New Mexico | Black Shuck co-operative
